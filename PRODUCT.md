# Impact Murals — Product and Positioning Context

## Core positioning
Impact Murals is an art direction and production studio creating site-specific art for brands, spaces, developments, destinations, and the public realm.

It is not simply a mural supplier and not a general creative agency.

Its value begins before execution:
- understanding the project context;
- defining the role art should play;
- shaping the creative direction;
- selecting the appropriate artistic language or artist;
- adapting the response to the site, audience, brand, and operational constraints;
- managing production and final delivery.

Large-scale murals are a major capability and a strong source of credibility, but they are not the entire positioning.

## Desired visitor perception
Within a few seconds, a qualified visitor should think:

> This is not only a company that paints walls. They can understand our project, define the right artistic response, and deliver it at scale.

## Main audiences
- Creative Directors
- Heads of Brand and Marketing
- Experiential and event agencies
- Architects and interior designers
- Developers and real-estate groups
- Hospitality and retail decision-makers
- Municipalities and public-realm stakeholders
- Organizations commissioning public art
- Project managers seeking a reliable creative-production partner

## Main commercial contexts
### Public Art & Large-Scale Murals
Façades, public spaces, developments, destinations, infrastructure, urban surfaces, and large visible interventions.

### Brand & Retail Environments
Retail spaces, offices, campaigns, branded environments, activations, and physical experiences that translate brand identity into a place.

### Hospitality, Property & Destinations
Hotels, restaurants, developments, communities, shared environments, and destination-led projects requiring a distinctive sense of place.

Art activations may sit mainly inside Brand & Retail rather than appearing as a disconnected fourth business.

## What Impact Murals can take responsibility for
- Creative direction
- Concept development
- Artist curation
- Artwork development
- Site-specific adaptation
- Murals and art installations
- Large-scale production
- Artist coordination
- Materials and access planning
- On-site execution and final delivery

These are not ten disconnected products. The website should communicate one continuous responsibility from early direction to real-world completion.

## Website role
The site serves two commercial systems.

### Cold email
A prospect receives an outbound message, checks the website, and must quickly see a credible, premium, relevant studio capable of handling serious projects.

### Organic search
Future pages will target high-intent searches around:
- public art UAE / Dubai;
- large-scale murals;
- mural production for agencies;
- brand environments;
- retail art;
- hospitality art;
- art activations;
- place identity;
- public-realm art production.

The landing page remains the premium brand and conversion experience. Future SEO pages operate as targeted entry points using the same design system.

## Primary CTA
`DISCUSS A PROJECT`

It must appear:
- in the navigation;
- in the first viewport;
- after the role of Impact Murals becomes clear;
- in the final conversion sequence.

## Secondary CTA
`VIEW SELECTED WORK`

## Contact framing
The site must welcome early-stage enquiries, not only finished tenders.

Preferred framing:

> Have a location, a brief, or simply an early idea?

Avoid weak CTA language such as:
- Learn More
- Contact Us
- Get Started
- Discover More

## Tone
- Calm
- Confident
- Clear
- Commercial without sounding aggressive
- Creative without becoming mystical
- Premium without inflated claims
- Precise but not contractual

## The website must not feel like
- a basic mural company;
- a personal graffiti portfolio;
- a generic full-service agency;
- a SaaS landing page;
- an art site so contemplative that the offer becomes unclear;
- a collection of unrelated trendy components.
