## Guardrails
- Do not create mass near-duplicate pages.
- Do not publish thin AI-generated location variants.
- Do not hide SEO pages from users while exposing them only to crawlers.
- Each commercial page must provide genuine value and relevant proof.
- Case studies and first-hand production expertise should support pillar pages.
