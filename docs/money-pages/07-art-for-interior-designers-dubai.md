### 11 · Questions, guides and the professional brief

**Purpose:** answer use-and-decision questions and invite an incomplete brief.

**Headline (FAQ):** QUESTIONS DESIGN TEAMS ASK.

**FAQ**

1. **Can you join at concept stage?** Yes. Early involvement lets the art shape the scheme instead of being fitted into it. We help identify where art can do the most and which formats and artists suit it.
2. **Can you execute a design we have already approved?** Yes. We confirm feasibility, adapt the design to the real surface and plan the production. The design intent stays with your studio.
3. **How do you choose the artist for our scheme?** We propose the artist whose language fits it, with our reasons, and you approve. If your client already has an artist in mind, we work within that direction and focus on development and production.
4. **Who approves what, you or the client?** The designer and the client approve the artistic direction, the client and the project manager approve budget and programme, and the contractor approves technical interfaces. The scope fixes the review gates.
5. **Can you work with an art or FF&E consultant?** Yes. If they hold curation, we sit inside their package and take direction from them.
6. **Who coordinates installation?** The scope states it. We coordinate the artistic installation with your contractor and state what we need from other trades.
7. **Do we pay for concepts?** Project-specific concepts and mockups are developed after appointment, inside the paid scope. Before that we discuss feasibility and scope with you.
8. **Do we have to name the client?** No. You can start without naming the client or the project. Share what you are comfortable with.
9. **Can several projects stay consistent?** A direction can be set once and applied across several sites with local adaptation. See Multi-site Art Programmes.

**Headline (guides):** FOR THE DESIGN FILE.

**Final brief, copy:** Tell us the project type, the stage, and where art might sit. Plans, elevations and references help. If you do not know yet who appoints the art, say that too.
