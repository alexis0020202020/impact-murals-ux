**Why it is specified now.** The Strategy Master still supports this page after the core set: dedicated corporate pages, mobile venue delivery and group formats exist in the market, and Impact already has an artist-led workshop offer. It stays a **narrow corporate and group page**. A broad `/art-workshops-dubai/` page remains a no-go, and no consumer class, kids' lesson, paint-and-sip or graffiti-workshop SEO is created.

**Launch gate (all five, confirmed by the studio):**

1. Facilitator availability, and who leads which format.
2. Realistic group sizes, with how many assistants each size needs.
3. Setup, materials and clean-up labour per session.
4. Delivery economics, including a minimum worthwhile engagement, so a standard offer can be set.
5. One documented pilot or real session (setup, participation, finished output).

The copy below makes **no capacity, price or timing claim**, so it is true whichever way these come out.

### 2 · Format and output choices

**Purpose:** show the formats by what the group ends up with, and separate workshops from passive performance.

**Headline:** FOUR KINDS OF OUTPUT.

**Copy** (ruled rows)

- A SPECIALIST SESSION. Lettering or calligraphy, or another technique the artist leads, where it suits the group.

Below: A workshop is taught. If guests simply add to a shared wall at an event, that is Participatory Art. If you want to watch an artist paint, that is Live Painting.

### 7 · Process and fee factors

**Purpose:** show the path and the cost variables without a per-head price.

**Headline:** HOW A SESSION COMES TOGETHER.

**Copy**

1. BRIEF. Objective, group, date and venue.
2. FORMAT AND FEASIBILITY. Format, numbers and staffing checked.
3. APPOINTMENT. Scope agreed.
4. SESSION DESIGN. The session and the artwork brief developed.
5. PREPARATION. Materials, setup and the room.
6. THE SESSION AND THE OUTPUT. Led by the artist, finished and handed over.

COST DEPENDS ON the size of the group, the length of the session, the complexity of the format, the number of assistants, the materials, the venue and the cost of the final work.

We do not publish a per-head price, because group size, format and the finished work change the costs together.

**Composition:** the shared numbered ledger, then the fee line in a quiet two-column block.

### 8 · Proof, questions and enquiry

**Purpose:** answer organiser questions and invite a partial brief.

**Selected sessions: CONDITIONAL.** No documented session exists. The module is **omitted** until a pilot or a real session can be shown (setup, participation, finished output), up to three factual cards. Corporate case studies are optional.

**Headline (FAQ):** ORGANISER QUESTIONS.

**FAQ**

1. **Do participants need experience?** No. The session is built so a first-time painter can take part, and the artist adjusts the pace to the room.
2. **How many people can take part?** It depends on the format, the room and the number of assistants. We confirm the numbers after a staffing and space review. We do not promise every format for every group size.
3. **Is it a fixed session or a drop-in?** Either can be planned. A fixed session suits a team; a drop-in suits a larger event. The duration is set with you.
4. **Can the session reflect our brand or theme?** Yes. The theme, the palette or a campaign idea can shape the brief, and the artist keeps the result coherent.
5. **Do you bring the materials and set up?** The proposal states what is supplied, the setup and the clean-up. Where the venue has rules, we plan inside them.
6. **What happens to the artwork afterwards?** It can be displayed, assembled, framed, taken home or installed. Anything beyond the session itself is agreed as separate scope.
7. **Do we pay for session design before we commit?** Session design follows appointment and sits inside the agreed scope. Before that we confirm the format, the numbers and the venue with you.
8. **Can you accommodate language and access needs?** Tell us early. They are planned into the format, the room and the pace.

**Headline (guides):** SESSION IDEAS AND PLANNING.

**Six guides** (see section E).

**Final brief, headline:** WHO IS IN THE ROOM?

**Final brief, copy:** Who is taking part, roughly how many, the date if you have one and the venue. Say whether you want a shared piece or individual ones. Details can be rough.

**Composition:** shared closing pattern.
