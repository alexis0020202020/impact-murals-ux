### 6 · Materials, lifespan and care

**Purpose:** answer durability honestly.

**Headline:** MADE TO STAND OUTDOORS.

**Copy**

The substrate sets the preparation, the paint system, the finish and the durability. Outdoor work also faces heat, sun, dust and cleaning. We match the system to the surface and the expected life, and write down how the work should be maintained.

We do not offer a universal warranty, and we say plainly what a given system can and cannot be expected to do.

**Composition:** a three-frame preparation sequence (bare wall, prepared surface, painted) at one height; the two paragraphs to its right.

**Media:** ESSENTIAL: a genuine preparation, sample and detail sequence. EXISTS in part (`EXTERIOR_MURAL_BEFORE` for the bare wall). TO SOURCE for preparation and finish detail. No material-specific note appears without a real sample.

### 11 · Budget factors and questions

**Purpose:** explain budget logic and answer procurement questions.

**Headline (budget):** WHAT A PUBLIC ART BUDGET DEPENDS ON.

**Copy**

The artist and the design. The area and level of detail. The surface condition. Access. The public interface. Specialists. Programme. Expected life and care. Multi-site logistics.

We do not quote a rate per square metre, because the surface, the height and the site matter more than the area. Send the site and whatever you already know.

**Headline (FAQ):** QUESTIONS FOR THE BRIEF.

**FAQ**

1. **Do we need to have chosen an artist?** No. We can develop the direction and select the artist, work from artwork you have approved, or take a selected artist or a tender package through production planning.
2. **Who do you work with?** Developers, destinations, venues, institutions, agencies and direct commissioners. The scope is whatever the site needs, from concept to production.
3. **Can you deliver façade and large-scale work?** Yes, planned around the surface, the height and the access. We define the artistic access requirement and coordinate the contractor or specialist provider who supplies the equipment.
4. **Who obtains the approvals?** The party responsible for each approval stays responsible for it. We prepare the artistic information an approval needs. We do not promise to obtain every permit.
5. **How is public circulation protected?** Public separation, working hours and daily clean-down are planned with the site operator, and the responsible party for each measure is named in the scope.
6. **How long does exterior work last?** It depends on the surface, the paint system, the exposure and the care. We state the expected life of the proposed system and write down maintenance. There is no blanket warranty.
7. **Can you handle several sites, or a temporary work?** Yes. Several sites share one direction with local adaptation. A temporary work is specified for its duration. See Multi-site Public Art and, for dimensional work, Art Installations.
8. **Do we see a concept before we appoint you?** No. The concept is the first piece of creative work in the agreed paid scope, so it follows appointment.
9. **Who holds the rights, and is the artist credited?** Authorship, usage rights and credit are agreed in the commission. We do not assume them.

**Composition:** the budget text as a single quiet block; the FAQ as one accordion with every answer in the HTML.
