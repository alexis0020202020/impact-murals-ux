# Quality Gates

## Gate 1 — Identity spine
Before building the full page, confirm:
- the hero is readable within five seconds;
- `DISCUSS A PROJECT` is immediately visible;
- the logo animation is controlled and not gimmicky;
- the Impact Field is perceptible but not literal;
- the hero-to-work transition is bespoke;
- the first project frame feels like a physical surface, not a card;
- mobile is a designed composition, not a compressed desktop version;
- the page already feels distinctive with placeholders.

If any of these fail, refine the identity spine before expanding the landing.

## Gate 2 — Complete landing
Confirm:
- the three contexts are clearly different without becoming separate mini-sites;
- context chapters and deeper case-study proof are not repetitive;
- the end-to-end role is understandable without reading a contract;
- operational delivery is credible;
- editable content remains outside layout components;
- no navigation link is broken;
- no claim is invented;
- all motion has reduced-motion fallbacks;
- mobile, tablet, laptop, and desktop are functional;
- build and type checks pass.

## Gate 3 — Production readiness later
Not part of initial V1 unless explicitly requested:
- real form delivery;
- final client logos;
- final legal/privacy content;
- analytics and consent;
- Search Console setup;
- final production vector logo;
- deployment and domain switch.
